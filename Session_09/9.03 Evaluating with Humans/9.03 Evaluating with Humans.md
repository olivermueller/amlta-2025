# Evaluating with Humans

## Chatbot Arena

The most common way to evaluate with humans is something like chatbot arenas or LLM arenas, e.g., [LMSys's Chatbot Arena](https://lmarena.ai/de) [(Chiang et al., 2024)](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2403.04132). These allow to calculate wins over other LLMs and allow an aggregated view which LLM is preferred by humans.

An example for that is given in the `chatbot_arena.py`. I will show it to you in our session.

## Evaluation in other GUIs

Another possibility is the [OpenWebUI tool](https://docs.openwebui.com/) that enables to integrate Open-source and proprietary LLMs in a ChatGPT-like environment ready for evaluations. This can also track answers as shown in our session. It also enables the integration of RAG, Agents and tools using the GUI but also additional pipelines from other LLM providers via the [Pipelines](https://docs.openwebui.com/features/pipelines/) framework.

## Experimental Evaluation
Last but not least you can implement (field) experiments to really evaluate LLMs in a business environment. We will show this in our presentation on our research-in-progress with RAG LLMs in Auditing.
